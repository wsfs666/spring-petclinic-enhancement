# No-Show Policy

Policy ID: clinic-no-show-v1  
Category: appointments  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Definition

- A no-show occurs when an owner **does not arrive** for a confirmed appointment and **has not contacted the clinic by 15 minutes after** the scheduled start time.
- An appointment that the clinic has already confirmed as cancelled is **not** a no-show.
- A late owner who contacts the clinic and follows staff instructions is handled under the **Late Arrival Policy**, not automatically as a no-show.

## Fees and future appointments

- The standard no-show fee is **$40 per missed appointment**.
- A no-show fee is separate from the **$25 late-cancellation fee**; both are **not charged for the same missed appointment**.
- After **two no-shows within a rolling 12-month period**, the clinic may ask the owner to confirm future appointments by telephone. This does not automatically ban online booking.
- Staff may review exceptional circumstances and waive a fee at their discretion; a waiver is not guaranteed.

## How to avoid a no-show

- If unable to attend, contact the clinic before the visit and request a confirmed cancellation or reschedule.
- Sending a message to the AI assistant alone does **not** count as notifying the front desk.

## Example

If a 9:00 AM appointment is unattended and the owner has not contacted staff by 9:15 AM, the visit may be marked as a no-show and assessed the $40 fee.
