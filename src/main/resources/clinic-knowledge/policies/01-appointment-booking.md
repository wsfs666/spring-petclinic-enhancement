# Appointment Booking Policy

Policy ID: clinic-appointment-booking-v1  
Category: appointments  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Booking availability

- The demonstration clinic accepts routine appointments up to **60 calendar days in advance**.
- Online appointment requests must be made at least **2 hours before** the available start time.
- Each appointment is for **one pet and one available time slot**. Owners with multiple pets must book separate appointments.
- A time slot is confirmed only after the system successfully creates the appointment. Viewing an available slot or preparing a booking does **not** reserve it.
- Two owners cannot hold confirmed appointments for the same time slot. If a slot becomes unavailable during booking, the owner must choose another slot.

## Confirmation and changes

- A successful booking receives an appointment confirmation ID and scheduled date/time.
- Booking an appointment does not guarantee a specific veterinarian unless the clinic explicitly confirms one.
- To cancel, see the **Appointment Cancellation Policy**. To change the date or time, see the **Appointment Rescheduling Policy**.
- Online booking is for **routine care only**. It is not appropriate for emergencies; see the **Emergency Care Policy**.

## Example

An owner who sees a 10:00 AM slot but has not completed confirmation has not reserved it. Another owner may book that slot first.
