# Appointment Cancellation Policy

Policy ID: clinic-cancellation-v1  
Category: appointments  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Notice and fees

- Owners may cancel a confirmed routine appointment **24 hours or more before** its scheduled start with **no cancellation fee**.
- A cancellation requested **less than 24 hours before** the scheduled start incurs a **$25 late-cancellation fee**.
- The 24-hour cutoff is measured in actual elapsed hours, including nights, weekends, and holidays—not business hours.
- A request made **exactly 24 hours before** the appointment is on time and has **no fee**.

## How to cancel

- Contact the clinic's front desk to request cancellation. A chat message to the AI assistant **does not by itself cancel** an appointment.
- A cancellation takes effect only when the clinic confirms it. Keep the confirmation for reference.
- A confirmed cancellation is not a no-show; see the **No-Show Policy** for unattended appointments.

## Exceptions and boundaries

- A medically urgent situation may be reviewed by staff for a discretionary fee waiver, but a waiver is **not guaranteed**.
- This policy addresses cancellation of appointments, not refunds for clinical services already provided; see the **Payment Policy**.

## Example

For a Tuesday appointment at 10:00 AM, cancelling by Monday at 10:00 AM is free. Cancelling Monday at 10:01 AM falls inside the 24-hour window and normally costs $25.
