# Clinic Hours and Contact Policy

Policy ID: clinic-hours-v1  
Category: operations  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Routine operating hours

- **Monday–Friday:** 9:00 AM–5:00 PM.
- **Saturday:** 9:00 AM–1:00 PM.
- **Sunday:** Closed.
- Hours are in the clinic's **local time zone**.
- The clinic may close on published holidays or during unexpected disruptions. Holiday closures override the regular weekly schedule.

## Appointments and communication

- Routine appointments take place during operating hours and are subject to available slots.
- Owners should contact the front desk during operating hours for cancellation, rescheduling, billing, and record requests.
- Messages sent outside business hours may not be reviewed until the next business day. A message alone is not confirmation that an appointment has been changed.
- The cancellation and rescheduling notice periods are measured in **elapsed hours**, not office opening hours; see those policies for their 24-hour rules.

## Out-of-hours care

- This demonstration clinic is **not a 24-hour veterinary emergency facility**.
- Do not wait for the clinic to reopen when a pet may need urgent or emergency attention. Contact an appropriate emergency veterinary provider; see the **Emergency Care Policy**.

## Example

The clinic is open on Saturday morning but closed on Sunday. A Saturday 11:00 AM routine appointment is possible only if an appointment slot is available.
