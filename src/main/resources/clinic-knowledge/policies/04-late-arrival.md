# Late Arrival Policy

Policy ID: clinic-late-arrival-v1  
Category: appointments  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Arrival expectations

- Owners should arrive **10 minutes before** the scheduled appointment to allow time for check-in.
- Arrivals up to **10 minutes after** the scheduled start can usually be accommodated, although the visit may be shorter.
- For arrivals **more than 15 minutes late**, staff may require a new appointment if the remaining time is insufficient for appropriate care.
- For arrivals between **11 and 15 minutes late**, staff decide whether the visit can proceed based on the day's schedule.

## Communication and fees

- If running late, contact the front desk as soon as possible. Contacting staff does not guarantee the original slot can be kept.
- An owner who checks in or contacts staff and follows their instructions is **not automatically treated as a no-show** merely because the clinic reschedules the visit.
- If staff reschedule the visit because they cannot accommodate a late arrival, they determine whether a late-rescheduling fee applies under the **Appointment Rescheduling Policy**; no automatic fee is assessed by this policy.
- An owner who neither appears nor contacts the clinic by **15 minutes after** the start time may be classified as a no-show; see the **No-Show Policy**.

## Example

For a 2:00 PM appointment, 2:08 PM is within the usual grace window, while an arrival at 2:17 PM may require rescheduling.
