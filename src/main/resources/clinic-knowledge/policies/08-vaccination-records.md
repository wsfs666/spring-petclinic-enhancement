# Vaccination Records Policy

Policy ID: clinic-vaccination-records-v1  
Category: records  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Bringing vaccination history

- For a new pet or first visit, owners are encouraged to provide available vaccination history **at least 2 business days before** the appointment, or bring a copy at check-in if advance submission is not possible.
- Records should, when available, include the pet's name, vaccine names, administration dates, and issuing veterinary provider.
- The clinic reviews the records to avoid unnecessary duplicate vaccinations and to plan recommended preventive care.
- Missing paperwork **does not automatically cancel** an ordinary routine consultation. Staff may ask questions or arrange follow-up verification.

## Vaccination services

- Vaccine recommendations depend on the pet's age, health, lifestyle, prior history, and applicable local requirements. This demo policy does not declare one fixed vaccine schedule for every pet.
- A clinician must assess which vaccinations, if any, are appropriate. The AI assistant cannot determine medical eligibility or replace veterinary advice.
- If vaccination records are uncertain, staff may recommend contacting the prior provider; the clinic does not promise to administer a vaccine without sufficient history.

## Relation to other policies

- Asking the clinic for copies of records is covered by the **Medical Records and Privacy Policy**.
- To book a vaccination visit, follow the **Appointment Booking Policy** and select an available routine appointment.

## Example

An owner who cannot locate a past rabies certificate may still ask for a routine consultation, but a veterinarian must determine next steps for vaccination documentation.
