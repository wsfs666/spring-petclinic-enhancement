# Payment and Billing Policy

Policy ID: clinic-payment-v1  
Category: billing  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Payment for services

- Payment for routine veterinary services is due **at checkout on the day of the visit**, unless the clinic has approved a different arrangement in advance.
- The clinic accepts **major credit cards, debit cards, and cash** for in-person payment.
- An estimate can be requested before non-emergency treatment; the final charge depends on the services actually approved and performed.
- Owners must approve optional additional services before the clinic performs them whenever practical.

## Appointment-related fees

- Late cancellation **less than 24 hours before** a confirmed appointment: **$25**; see the **Appointment Cancellation Policy**.
- Late rescheduling **less than 24 hours before** the original appointment: **$25**, unless a stated waiver applies; see the **Appointment Rescheduling Policy**.
- No-show: **$40**; see the **No-Show Policy**.
- Cancellation and no-show fees are **not combined for the same appointment**.

## Insurance and refunds

- Pet insurance reimbursement is generally handled by the owner. The clinic can provide itemized receipts upon request but does not promise an insurer will reimburse any charge.
- Questions about an incorrect charge or a refund for services already delivered must be reviewed by the front desk. This policy does **not** guarantee a refund or specify a universal refund percentage.
- The AI assistant cannot collect card details or authorize refunds.

## Example

A completed routine visit is paid for at checkout. A separate late-cancellation fee applies only when an appointment is cancelled inside the 24-hour window.
