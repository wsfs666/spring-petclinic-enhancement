# Medical Records and Privacy Policy

Policy ID: clinic-medical-records-v1  
Category: records  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Requesting copies of records

- Owners may request copies of their pet's clinical records through the clinic's front desk.
- The clinic verifies the requester's identity and relationship to the pet before releasing records.
- Routine requests are generally processed within **5 business days** after the clinic receives the information needed to verify the request.
- For a time-sensitive transfer to another veterinarian, tell the front desk the destination and required deadline; expedited handling is evaluated case by case and is not guaranteed.

## Sharing and privacy

- The clinic shares clinical records with a third party only when permitted by applicable rules and its authorization procedures.
- Owners may be asked for written consent before non-routine disclosure to third parties.
- The AI assistant must **not** disclose confidential pet records, owner contact information, or account details merely because someone asks a question in chat.
- This document explains records handling; it does not grant the AI assistant access to a pet's private medical history.

## Corrections and interpretation

- Owners who find an error may ask staff to review it. An original clinical entry is not silently overwritten without appropriate review.
- Clinical results and treatment decisions should be interpreted by a licensed veterinary professional, not inferred from policy text.

## Example

A verified owner requesting a routine copy on Monday can generally expect processing within five business days, subject to completed verification.
