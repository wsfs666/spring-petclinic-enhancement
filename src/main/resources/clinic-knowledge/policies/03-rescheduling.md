# Appointment Rescheduling Policy

Policy ID: clinic-rescheduling-v1  
Category: appointments  
Version: 1  
Status: DEMO — fictional clinic policy; not a real clinic's rules

## Changing an appointment

- Owners may request a change to a confirmed routine appointment's date or time through the clinic's front desk.
- A reschedule requested **24 hours or more before** the original start time has **no rescheduling fee**.
- A reschedule requested **less than 24 hours before** the original start time normally incurs a **$25 late-rescheduling fee**.
- The deadline uses elapsed hours, including weekends and holidays. Exactly 24 hours' notice is on time.

## Availability and confirmation

- Rescheduling is **subject to an available replacement slot**. A request does not reserve the replacement slot until the clinic confirms the change.
- If no replacement time is available, the original appointment remains in place unless the clinic separately confirms its cancellation.
- Moving an appointment does not automatically produce a refund for treatment already delivered.
- The AI assistant may explain this policy, but a chat request alone **does not complete** a reschedule.

## Special cases

- If clinic staff ask an owner to reschedule because of the clinic's own staffing or scheduling problem, the owner is **not charged** a rescheduling fee.
- Arriving late is covered by the **Late Arrival Policy**. Failing to appear without timely contact is covered by the **No-Show Policy**.

## Example

For a Friday appointment at 3:00 PM, requesting a new time on Thursday at 3:00 PM is free; requesting it Thursday at 4:00 PM normally costs $25.
